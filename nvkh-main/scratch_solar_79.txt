22: ## Theme correctness
23: 
24: Both older token layers declare their aliases inside a `:root` block — `--base:
25: var(--mc-canvas)` in `tokens-bridge.css`, and a second `--theme-*` /
26: `--surface-*` / `--text-*` family in `premiumDesign.css`. A custom property's
27: `var()` is substituted **at the element where it is declared**, so every one of
28: those aliases froze against the dark palette on `<html>`. `.theme-solar` and
29: `.theme-contrast` redefine the `--mc-*` scale on `.shell`, one level below, and
30: therefore never reached them: Solar Light lit the panels but left the page
31: canvas near-black and several headings white on white.
32: 
33: `cockpit.css` re-declares both families verbatim on `.shell`, the element that
34: actually carries the theme class, so each alias resolves against whichever
35: palette is active. Only the declaration site moves, so the dark theme computes
36: exactly as before.
37: 
38: **If you add a token that other rules consume, declare it on `.shell`, not on
39: `:root`** — a `:root` declaration cannot follow the theme.
40: 
41: Selectors are scoped just tightly enough (`.shell .view-workspace …`,
42: `html body #root .shell`) to win over the historical `!important` rules without
43: escalating further. New rules should prefer tokens and avoid `!important` unless
44: overriding a legacy `!important` declaration.
